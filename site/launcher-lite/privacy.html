Privacy policy

Rusty Launcher Lite processes a small amount of device information locally so it can show and organize launchable apps. It does not transmit that information to Mesmer Prism or any third party.

Information used on the headset

No account or identity is created.

Launchable app information

Android supplies labels, package and activity names, and icons for enabled apps that publish a public launchable front door. Launcher Lite uses this only to display its catalogue and launch the app you select.

Ordinary Wi-Fi state

Launcher Lite reads only whether ordinary Wi-Fi is on or off, so it can evaluate an optional per-app preference. It does not read network names, passwords, addresses, traffic, or Wi-Fi debugging state, and it never changes Wi-Fi.

Local preferences

Favorites, bounded tags, the search value, and Any / Wi-Fi on / Wi-Fi off preferences are stored in Launcher Lite's private app data on the headset.

Collection and sharing

Launcher Lite contains no ads, analytics, telemetry, account service, server API, or Internet permission. Mesmer Prism does not collect, sell, rent, share, or use the locally processed information for advertising or profiling.

Spatial presentation candidate

The unpublished hybrid candidate uses Meta Spatial SDK to display the same local panel in immersive mode. It adds SDK display permissions, not a network service, account or data-upload route.

Retention and deletion

The local preferences remain until you remove them. Clear Launcher Lite's app data in headset settings or uninstall the app to delete them. Android owns installed-app information and Wi-Fi state; Launcher Lite reads the current values when needed rather than uploading a copy.

Children and purchases

Launcher Lite has no purchases, subscriptions, social features, user-generated content, advertising, or account system. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and support reports can be submitted through the Rusty Kiosk public issue tracker. Policy changes will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
