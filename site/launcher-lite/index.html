For Meta Quest

One lightweight place to find and launch your apps.

Rusty Launcher Lite is a standalone native 2D app library. Browse launchable apps already installed on your headset, search by name or tag, keep favorites, and open app details before launching.

No companion app, Developer Mode, ADB, PC, account, subscription, or setup is required.

What you need

  • A supported Meta Quest headset
  • Apps already installed on that headset

The source includes an unpublished 0.3.0 hybrid update candidate with window and immersive presentations; Store availability and headset validation are separate.

Useful on its own

Everything here works immediately after installing Launcher Lite.

Browse and search

See apps that publish a normal launchable front door. Search by app name, package, or a local tag.

Favorites and tags

Keep a shorter favorites view and add or remove bounded local tags. State stays on your headset.

Wi-Fi preflight

Optionally prefer Wi-Fi on or off for an app. Lite explains a mismatch and can open standard Wi-Fi settings; it never changes Wi-Fi itself.

Help

Why is an installed app absent?

Launcher Lite can show only enabled, exported activities that Android returns for its fixed launcher queries. Some system or private activities intentionally do not publish a public front door.

Where are favorites and tags stored?

They stay in Launcher Lite's app-private data on this headset. Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove them.

Does Launcher Lite change Wi-Fi?

No. It only reads whether ordinary Wi-Fi is on. When a preference is unmet, you may cancel or open Android's standard Wi-Fi settings.

Do I need the full Rusty Kiosk?

No. The full Kiosk is an optional separate project for advanced lab workflows. Launcher Lite does not install it and never requires it.
